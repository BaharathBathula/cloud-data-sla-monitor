## Use Cases

## Enterprise Use Cases

This framework is designed to be applicable across industries where data reliability directly impacts decision-making, compliance, and operational efficiency.

--

## Finance and Accounting
In financial reporting environments, late or incomplete data can delay close processes and introduce regulatory risk.

## Example SLAs:
- Timeliness SLA: Daily financial datasets available by a fixed cutoff time
- Completeness SLA: No critical financial fields exceeding null thresholds

## Value:
- Prevents delayed close cycles
- Improves confidence in financial reporting
- Enables audit-ready data reliability evidence

--

## E-commerce and Digital Platforms
E-commerce platforms rely on fresh and accurate data for pricing, inventory, and customer analytics.

## Example SLAs:
- Freshness SLA: Pricing and inventory datasets updated within defined intervals
- Volume SLA: Expected record counts for transactional data

## Value:
- Reduces customer-facing errors
- Prevents stale analytics and dashboards
- Improves operational responsiveness

--

## Healthcare and Regulated Domains
In healthcare and regulated industries, data completeness and timeliness are critical for compliance and operational safety.

## Example SLAs:
- Completeness SLA: Required clinical or operational fields populated
- Timeliness SLA: Data availability within mandated reporting windows

## Value:
- Supports compliance requirements
- Reduces manual validation effort
- Enables traceable data reliability controls

--

## Enterprise Analytics Platforms
Large organizations with multiple analytics teams often struggle with inconsistent data quality standards.

## Example SLAs:
- Standardized SLA definitions across datasets
- Consistent validation rules applied enterprise-wide

## Value:
- Establishes common reliability standards
- Improves trust in shared analytics assets
- Reduces downstream data incidents
